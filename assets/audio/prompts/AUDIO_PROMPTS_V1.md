# HERO Audio Prompts V1

## 공통 BGM Prefix

> Instrumental only. Mobile game background music for a nuclear human-performance training simulation. Restrained tactical mood, clear midrange for text/dialogue readability, no vocals, no sirens, no alarms, no emergency broadcast tones, no imitation of industrial warning signals, moderate dynamics, no sudden peaks.

## BGM-01 Campaign Map
Calm strategic mission-preparation background. 82 BPM, restrained hybrid acoustic/electronic texture, subtle pulse, confident but not heroic, seamless-loop-friendly, 60–90 seconds.

## BGM-02 Briefing
Technical anticipation before a maintenance task. Sparse instrumentation, 76 BPM, low cognitive load, neutral tension, seamless-loop-friendly, 45–60 seconds.

## BGM-03 Operation Base
Focused technical workplace tension. Subtle electronic pulse and muted percussion, 82 BPM, calm but alert, sparse arrangement, designed to loop under dialogue, 60–90 seconds.

## BGM-04 Operation Tension
Subtle escalation without alarm language. Add texture and harmonic tension rather than sirens or sharp rhythmic warning patterns. 84–92 BPM, 45–60 seconds.

## BGM-05 Reflective Review
Reflective and neutral post-scenario music. Gentle resolution, learning-and-recovery tone, not sad or triumphant, 60–90 seconds.

## Stingers
- Safe stop: affirmative but restrained, 3–6 seconds.
- Near miss: brief suspended resolution, 3–6 seconds.
- Event: sober, neutral closure, no disaster/cinematic alarm language, 3–6 seconds.

## 공통 SFX Prefix

> Short abstract UI sound effect for a training game. Soft transient, non-alarming, no siren, no bell pattern associated with real equipment, no voice, no realistic industrial alarm.

## SFX cues
- UI tap: 0.15–0.3 s, soft tactile digital tick.
- Choice confirm: 0.25–0.5 s, neutral confirmation.
- Card draw: 0.3–0.8 s, light paper/digital swipe hybrid.
- Card use: 0.4–0.9 s, restrained protective shimmer, no fantasy explosion.
- Info open: 0.2–0.5 s, subtle document reveal.
- Time advance: 0.2–0.6 s, single movement cue; no repeated clock ticking.
- Event attention: 0.3–0.8 s, abstract attention cue; explicitly not alarm-like.
- Barrier weaken: 0.4–0.8 s, muted tonal descent.
- Barrier reinforce: 0.4–0.8 s, muted tonal resolve.
- Result stamp: 0.3–0.7 s, soft physical/digital stamp.
- Rank update: 0.3–0.6 s, restrained progress cue.
- Chapter unlock: 0.5–1.0 s, short neutral-open cue.
